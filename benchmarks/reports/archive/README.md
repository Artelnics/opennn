# Historical benchmark reports

The September 11 snapshot preserves the seven previous reports byte for byte.
It records what was written before the consolidation, including statements
whose supporting evidence is now marked incomplete or unsuitable for the next
release.

Use the [current publication review](../publication-review.md) for readiness.
Do not reuse an archived "passing" label or headline without checking its raw
measurements and the current protocol. Original relative paths and commands
describe the earlier layout; the local relocation manifest maps the old result
paths to their preserved archive locations.

The report snapshot hashes are retained in the ignored results archive at
`benchmarks/results/archive/2026-09-11/report-snapshot-hashes.json`.
