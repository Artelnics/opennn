# Contributing to OpenNN

Base contributions on `dev`, include the validation you ran, and describe the
resulting behavior in the pull request. [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) holds the
engineering rules, verification commands and release procedure.

## Contributor License Agreement

OpenNN is offered under the GNU AGPL and under commercial licences
([LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)). To keep both possible, a pull request can be
merged only after its author has accepted the
[Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). You keep the copyright in your
contribution; the agreement is a licence, not an assignment.

To accept it, fill in the signature table of [CLA.md](CLA.md), including every
Git name and email you commit from, and send it to artelnics@artelnics.com.
You sign it once. If your employer may own what you write, your employer must
authorise the contribution, as section 4 of the agreement explains.

Employees and interns of Artificial Intelligence Techniques, S.L. contribute
under their own agreements with the company and do not sign the CLA.

## Reporting problems

For a bug report, include the OpenNN commit, operating system, compiler,
CPU/CUDA configuration, reproduction command and error, and file it in
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Artelnics/opennn/issues). Report security
vulnerabilities privately to artelnics@artelnics.com instead.
